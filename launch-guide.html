Launch Guide & Growth Plan — prepared August 2026

1️⃣ Get Paid Online (start today, Rs 0 cost)

Phase 1 — This week, free
1

Set up your Raast ID. Open your bank's app → find "Raast" → register your mobile number 0336 7889788 as your Raast ID. Now any customer in Pakistan can send money instantly and free from any bank app using just your number. This is the single best free payment upgrade in Pakistan.

2

Add your IBAN to the website. Open index.html, find the BANK settings near the top of the script, and fill in your bank name, account title and IBAN. Every WhatsApp order will then automatically include your payment details.

3

Activate Easypaisa or JazzCash on 0336 7889788 (if not already). Many customers prefer wallets — accept them and ask for a screenshot.

4

Keep Cash on Delivery. In Pakistan, COD still closes the most orders. Confirm every COD order on WhatsApp before dispatch to avoid fake orders.

Phase 2 — When orders grow: card payments

Safepay (safepay.pk) is the best fit when you're ready for debit/credit card payments on the website. As a sole proprietor you don't need a registered company — you need: your CNIC copy, a bank account maintenance certificate (ask your bank branch, usually issued same day), and a simple declaration of sole proprietorship on a letterhead. Fees ~1.5–2.5% per transaction, no setup or annual fee. Alternatives: XPay by PostEx, PayPro, GoPayFast.

⚠️ Skip bank gateways (HBL/UBL/Alfalah) for now — they have setup + annual fees and slower onboarding. Fintech gateways are free to start.

2️⃣ Put the Website Online (cheapest path)

1

Free hosting on Netlify. Go to netlify.com → sign up free → "Add new site" → "Deploy manually" → drag the sami fish folder (with index.html inside) onto the page. Your store is live in ~30 seconds at a link like samifishes.netlify.app. Total cost: Rs 0. (Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages are equally good free options.)

2

Buy your domain — samifishes.com is available! (checked August 2026). Buy it on Namecheap or Cloudflare for ≈ $10–12/year (~Rs 3,000–3,500). In Netlify: Site settings → Domain management → add custom domain → follow the DNS steps. A .pk domain (samifishes.pk via pk5.pknic.net.pk) costs ~Rs 3,500/2 years if you prefer local branding.

3

Update your socials. Put the link in your Instagram bio, Facebook group description, and WhatsApp Business profile. Every post caption should end with the link.

Total running cost: ~Rs 3,000 per YEAR (domain only). No monthly fees — compare that to Shopify at ~Rs 90,000/year.

3️⃣ Your Admin Panel (update products from your phone)

The store has a built-in Google Sheets admin system — the Sheet becomes your control panel, editable from your phone, no coding ever:

1

Create a Google Sheet and import products-template.csv (File → Import). Columns: id, category, name, price, emoji, tag, description, img.

2

In the Sheet: File → Share → Publish to web → choose the sheet → format CSV → Publish → copy the link.

3

Paste that link into SHEET_CSV_URL near the top of index.html (between the quotes) and re-upload to Netlify once.

4

Done forever: add a row = new product appears on the site. Change a price in the Sheet and the site updates. Put a photo URL in the img column to replace the emoji with a real photo.

💡 Free product photo hosting: upload photos to postimages.org and paste the "direct link" into the img column.

4️⃣ Instagram → Website (the honest answer)

Fully-automatic product import from Instagram isn't practical for a small store — Meta retired the simple Instagram API; the current one needs a Business account, a registered Meta developer app, app review, and a paid server running 24/7. That's overkill and fragile.

What I set up instead (best of both):

• The website has an Instagram section. Create a free feed widget at lightwidget.com or behold.so for @sami_fishes_aquariums, paste the embed code into INSTAGRAM_EMBED_HTML in index.html — then your latest posts appear on the site automatically every time you post.
• For sellable products, the Google Sheet takes ~30 seconds per product from your phone — post to Instagram, then add the row. Reliable, free, and you control prices.

5️⃣ Branding Review (Instagram + Facebook)

What's working

✅ Clear niche and a memorable logo (blue + goldfish orange — now also your website theme). ✅ Consistent name across IG and FB. ✅ "Beauty of Betta" is a great content franchise — keep naming collections.

What needs fixing

🔧 Facebook: you have a Group (31 members) but a business needs a Page. A Page gets you: a "Shop Now"/WhatsApp button, reviews, Instagram linking, ads capability, and appearing in search. Create the Page "Sami Fishes & Aquariums", link Instagram to it, and keep the group as your community space ("Fish Keepers Lahore by Sami") — groups are for discussion, Pages are your shopfront. Also fix the group description: "Only Fishes" → write what you sell, your city, WhatsApp number and website link.

🔧 Instagram bio: add the WhatsApp number and (once live) the website link — right now there's no way to buy from your bio. Fix the typo "OFFICAL" → "OFFICIAL" in the profile name.

🔧 Post rhythm: 3–4 posts/week beats 10 posts one week and silence the next. Formula that works for fish sellers: Mon = new arrival reel, Wed = care tip (builds trust), Fri = customer tank photo or before/after, Sun = offer/bundle. Reels of feeding time and bag-opening of new stock consistently outperform photos.

🔧 Always price in captions or "DM for price"? Put prices ON the website and say "price & order link in bio" — this trains buyers to visit your store instead of 50 unanswered DMs.

6️⃣ Things You Didn't Ask About (but matter)

WhatsApp Business app (free) — switch your 0336 7889788 to it: business profile with catalog, auto-greeting, away messages, and order labels. Your store's checkout already opens straight into your WhatsApp chat.

Google Business Profile (free) — register "Sami Fishes & Aquariums" with your Lahore location so you appear on Google Maps when people search "aquarium shop near me". This brings more local buyers than any ad.

Live fish delivery policy — publish it: "Live arrival guaranteed. Unboxing video required for claims." Protects you from fake dead-on-arrival claims and reassures serious buyers.

Order record keeping — keep a second tab in your Google Sheet: date, customer, items, amount, payment method, status. At month-end you'll see your best sellers instantly.

Delivery beyond Lahore — when ready, partner with Trax / PostEx / M&P for equipment (COD supported nationwide); live fish via train/bus cargo with oxygen bags is standard practice between cities.
